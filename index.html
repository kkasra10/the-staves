THE-Staves

music notation plotter

Pick a note or rest, then tap a stave at the pitch you want – it's added after the last symbol (or after the selected one). Bars fill up and split automatically from the time signature; overfull bars are tinted red. Tap a symbol to select it.
1–9 breve…128th notes · Shift+1–9 rests · . dot · ↑↓ change pitch · ←→ move selection · ⌫ delete · E eraser · ⌘/Ctrl Z undo
Library symbols go where notation puts them: accidentals before the note, dynamics and pedal marks below, fermatas and ornaments above, clefs and signatures on the stave. Tap near a note to attach one; drag sideways to move it; ▲▼ nudge, A−/A+ resize.
